200K-Rip
A sports card ripping game
Inspired by 82-0.com — but for cards.

Rip four packs.
Chase $200K.

Every box gives you four sealed packs, six cards each. The game rolls a random sport, year, and brand for every pack, then you rip it and see what you got. Base cards are worth pennies. A 1-of-1 Superfractor can be worth millions.

You keep all 24 cards, but only five get graded. Pick your best, send them to PSA, and try to clear $200,000 in combined value.

Yes, Northampton Town is in there as its own sport — they're my favorite team.

A game by Drew Siegel.

01
Spin
Four wheels roll the pack: sport, year, brand, and what kind of hit you're chasing. Hate the roll? You get 2 re-spins per box.
02
Rip
The pack tears open and six cards fly out. Tap any card to lock it as one of your five keepers — but once locked, it's locked. Choose carefully; the next pack might be better.
03
Grade
Your five slabs get PSA grades (7 through 10 — gem mints multiply the value). Clear $200,000 combined and you win the box.
